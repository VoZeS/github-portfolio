A litle bit about me

Video games entered early in my life, with the classics Nintendo DS and PlayStation 3. My big brother made me understand the fun of playing alongside other people and, when I was older, suggested me to read Mark Fisher (The Weird and the Eerie), who introduced me into the good weirdness of the world, encouraging me to think over the things that we don't know and embrace this feeling.

When I finished my degree in Video Game Design and Development degree in the CITM, Terrassa (Barcelona). I joined Univrse, a VR company, working as a QA. In there, I developed QA Tools that helped with the struggles to collect data to analyse it and I managed the reports, communication and the software used to ensure a good workflow. In this company I helped bring down experiences as Black Mirror VR, winner of Special Jury Mention at the 2026 Cannes Film Festival Immersive Competition and Bodyverse, an immersive journey that reveals the science -–and the stories, behind what it means to be human; among +10 projects that I work on.

I'm always looking to evolve and ambrace new challenges, so that didn't stop me to create my own video games in my free time with Ginuh Games, a company I founded alongside Erik Martín and Juande García, two friends who wanted, as me, create immortal memories for the people.
