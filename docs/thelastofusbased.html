The Level: Abandoned power plant

Design intentions and goals

In this level, the player will have a clear objective: find a first aid kit (or the best they can find) in order to patch their wound. During this level, the player will encounter human enemies that will be patrolling the site, but due to their wound the player will only be able to kill the enemies from the back with the knife they are carrying.

Narrative and player objectives

Once the player escapes the woods, they encounter an abandoned power plant. This will be secured by some little group of ex-militants that established their camp some days ago. For the player’s luck, they carry a lot of medical supplies. These will be in a well secured room that will need power so the player can access, since the rainy days cutted the light off. The player will have to find the first aid kit, heal their wounds and escape from there in order to reach the lighthouse, situated a few miles away.

Gameplay

This level will feature stealthy combat, loot and solving puzzles in order to achieve the player’s objective.

Unique game elements

  • Stealth human combat.
  • Loot, related with environment puzzles (keys, gears…) to progress.
